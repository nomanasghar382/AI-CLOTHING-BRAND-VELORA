# Customer Manual

## Getting Started

1. Visit the VELORA storefront
2. Register an account or sign in
3. Browse the catalog using filters and instant search
4. Add items to cart or wishlist

## Shopping

- View product details including ethical passport information
- Select size and color variants
- Apply coupons at checkout
- Choose shipping method and enter address
- Complete payment via Stripe

## Orders

- Track order status in your account
- Download invoice (HTML/PDF)
- Request returns for eligible orders
- Receive email and in-app notifications for status updates

## AI Style

- Complete the style quiz to build your AI profile
- Get personalized outfit recommendations
- Chat with the style assistant
- Save outfits and provide feedback

## Wardrobe & Visual Search

- Add owned items to your digital wardrobe
- Get recommendations based on your wardrobe
- Upload an image for visual similarity search

## Loyalty & Rewards

- Earn points on purchases
- View VIP tier progress
- Redeem rewards and store credit
- Share referral codes with friends

## Community

- Browse community posts and look inspiration
- Like, comment, and bookmark posts
- Share your own outfit posts

## International Shopping

- Select your market and currency
- View shipping estimates for your region
- Track international shipments
