# VELORA User Guide

See [docs/release/USER_MANUAL.md](release/USER_MANUAL.md) and [docs/release/CUSTOMER_MANUAL.md](release/CUSTOMER_MANUAL.md) for complete documentation.

## Getting Started

1. Visit the VELORA storefront
2. Create an account or sign in with a demo account
3. Browse the catalog using filters and instant search

## Shopping

- Add items to your bag or wishlist
- Proceed to checkout with shipping and payment
- Track orders in your account dashboard
- Request returns for eligible items

## AI Style

- Complete the style quiz to build your AI profile
- Get personalized outfit recommendations
- Chat with the AI style assistant
- Save outfits and provide feedback

## Wardrobe & Visual Search

- Add owned items to your digital wardrobe
- Upload an image for visual similarity search
- Get recommendations based on your wardrobe

## Loyalty & Rewards

- Earn points on purchases
- View VIP tier progress
- Redeem rewards and store credit
- Share referral codes

## Community

- Browse community posts and outfit inspiration
- Like, comment, and bookmark posts
- Share your own styling thoughts

## International Shopping

- Select your market and currency
- View shipping estimates for your region
- Use the regional size guide
