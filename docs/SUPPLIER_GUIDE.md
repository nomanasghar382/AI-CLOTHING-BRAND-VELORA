# VELORA Supplier Guide

See [docs/release/SUPPLIER_MANUAL.md](release/SUPPLIER_MANUAL.md) for complete documentation.

## Profile

1. Sign in with a supplier account (`supplier@velora.test` / `VeloraDemo!2026`)
2. Complete company profile with contact details
3. Add warehouse addresses

## Inventory

- View synced inventory levels per product
- Sync inventory from your systems
- Adjust stock with audit trail

## Operations

- Manage purchase orders, shipments, and returns
- Upload certifications and compliance documents
- Review performance metrics and settlements

## Portal

Access the supplier portal at `/supplier` for inventory, orders, and performance dashboards.
